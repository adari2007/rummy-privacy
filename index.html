Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective immediately

🔒
No Data Collected

Rummy Score Book does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We have no servers, no accounts, and no way to identify you.

📱
Local Storage Only

All game data — player names, scores, saved games — is stored only on your device using the device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

🌐
No Internet Required

The app works completely offline. It makes no network requests of any kind. There are no analytics, no crash reporting services, and no third-party SDKs that phone home.

🚫
No Ads or Tracking

Rummy Score Book contains no advertisements and uses no tracking or analytics tools. We do not use cookies, device identifiers, or any form of behavioral tracking.

👶
Children's Privacy

Because we collect no data whatsoever, the app is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
